## AI disclosure — the two halves

*GM-facing. The short half is already in the paste block above; this section is
about the other half.*

The disclosure is deliberately split, because a recruitment post and a server pin
are read differently. Someone skimming eight adverts wants one sentence that
tells them whether to keep reading — a wall of text there costs you players who
would have been fine with the answer. Someone who stopped on that sentence
because they care wants the whole thing, and wants it somewhere permanent rather
than buried in a post that scrolls away.

So the post carries the sentence and the pin carries the detail: what the art
actually is, why no artist lost work over it, what the writing assistance does
and does not cover, and the standing offer of plain tokens.

**The pin text is in [[AI disclosure]]** — paste it once per server and leave it
pinned. It is written to be pasted verbatim; do not retype a fresh version per
game, because two disclosures that differ are worse than one that is blunt.

If a game genuinely departs from the standing statement — a bought painted map
that came out of an image generator, say — the honest fix is a line about that
map in this game's own post, not a rewritten pin.
